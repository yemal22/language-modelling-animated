How to explore the decks
  • Right arrow, Space or a click advances one build. Left arrow reverses it.
  • Page Down / Page Up change slides. O opens the slide overview. F toggles fullscreen.
  • Sliders and selectors change the teaching model without advancing the slide.
  • URLs save slide and build: #12/3. A plain #12 opens the completed slide.
  • N toggles the detailed explanation and source notes; the key is kept out of the slide chrome.
  • The brief white lead-in follows the reference style. Add ?blank=0 before the hash to disable it.
Attribution and teaching choices

Based on the supplied lecture From Counting Words to Pretrained Transformers by Shamsuddeen Muhammad, Google DeepMind Academic Fellow, Imperial College London. This is an independent study companion prepared for Morel KPAVODE, not an official Imperial course publication.

Visual direction: delamarifer’s Human–Machine Perception decks and our earlier EgoFalls presentation. The slide engine, editable SVG diagrams and computed examples here are new implementations.

Token vectors, gates and attention examples use explicitly chosen toy inputs and real calculations. They are not claimed to be measurements from a pretrained language model. The uploaded lecture PDF is credited but not redistributed.
