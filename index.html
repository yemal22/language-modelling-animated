How to explore the decks
  • Right arrow, Space or a click advances one build. Left arrow reverses it.
  • Page Down / Page Up change slides. O opens the slide overview. F toggles fullscreen.
  • Sliders and selectors change the worked example without advancing the slide.
  • URLs save slide and build: #12/3. A plain #12 opens the completed slide.
  • N toggles detailed explanations and source notes.
  • Add ?blank=0 before the hash to disable the white lead-in.
Sources and numerical examples

Based on the supplied lectures From Counting Words to Pretrained Transformers and From Counting Words to Pretrained Transformers (2) by Shamsuddeen Muhammad, Google DeepMind Academic Fellow, Imperial College London. This is an independent study companion prepared for Morel KPAVODE.

The architecture diagrams and teaching programs are original implementations. Chosen vectors, gates and weights produce exact worked calculations. Published-result charts separately identify the paper, table, metric and averaging rule. They do not show new experiments run for these decks. The supplied PDFs are credited but not redistributed.

Visual direction: delamarifer’s Human–Machine Perception decks and our earlier EgoFalls presentation.
